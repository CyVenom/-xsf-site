pipeline

One command, five stages.

A scan is a pipeline. Each stage is independently testable and async.

01

Crawler

Follows links, finds JS endpoints and hidden params.

02

Payload engine

Loads and ranks payloads by context.

03

Scanner

aiohttp and a bounded semaphore for concurrent requests.

04

Reflection analyzer

DOM parse, encoding check, false-positive filter.

05

Reporters

Console, JSON, HTML or CSV: same findings, four formats.

why it's different

A reflection is not a vulnerability.

Most scanners stop at the first match. xsf keeps going until it knows the match is exploitable.

a/naive-scanner
  • regex-matches the raw payload string
  • flags every reflection as a hit
  • no sense of which characters survived encoding
  • one evidence string, no exploitation notes
b/xsf✔
  • parses the live DOM to find the real context
  • scores confidence per context
  • fingerprints HTML, URL and JS-escape encoding
  • rates exploitation difficulty and explains why

cli

Five commands. No daemon to babysit.

$ xsf scan

Query params, forms, or a full crawl.

$ xsf crawl

Endpoints, forms, injectable parameters.

$ xsf payloads

List, inspect, export payload sets.

$ xsf report

Re-render a saved scan as HTML or CSV.

$ xsf config

Init, show, validate scan config.

$ xsf scan "$TARGET" --forms --waf-bypass

Everything on: forms, plus the WAF-bypass payload set.
